Dastgah-e Nava Tuning Information
Source: https://tuning.ableton.com/persian-radif/dastgah-e-nava/

Scale Degrees and Functions
  First Scale Degree (Tonic): D
  Second Degree: E koron (quarter-tone lowered)
    Function: Ist (pitch serving as ending note for phrases other than final cadences)
  Third Degree: F
    Function: Aqaz (beginning pitch where improvisation usually initiates)
  Fourth Degree: G
    Function: Finalis/Foroud (melodic cadence goal, conclusion tone)

Historical Context
  Dastgah-e Nava has a close relationship with Shur, and many of its Gushe-ha (sub-modes)
  are structured in the modal scheme of Shur.

  The modal scheme of Dastgah-e Nava is similar to the Maqam-e Nava, one of the maqams
  described by the 11th-century music theorist Muhammad Nishaburi.

Technical Details
  Uses the koron accidental, designed by Ali-Naqi Vaziri (1886-1979), which lowers
  pitch by a quarter-tone. The page indicates a defined "area of melodic motion" but
  provides no specific cent values or frequency ratios.
