Dastgah-e Homayun Tuning Information
Source: https://tuning.ableton.com/persian-radif/dastgah-e-homayun/

Scale Degrees and Functions
  First Degree (Aqaz): E koron
    Function: Starting point of improvisation, "beginning"
  Second Degree (Ist): F
    Function: Often serves as ending note for phrases (other than final cadences)
  Third Degree (Finalis/Foroud): G
    Function: Melodic cadence goal, creates sense of conclusion
  Fourth Degree (Shahed): A koron
    Function: Remarkably prominent pitch during music creation

Tuning Characteristics
  Koron symbol: Accidental that lowers pitch by a quarter-tone
  Designer of koron notation: Ali-Naqi Vaziri (1886-1979)
  Modal Structure: Similar to Dastgah-e Shur
  Modulation capability: Via sub-modes (Gushe-ha), can modulate to Segah or Chahargah

Cultural Context
  The music for zourkhaneh rituals, an Iranian traditional system of athletics
  and a form of martial arts, is usually sung or performed in this Dastgah.
  Known for its "royal mood."
