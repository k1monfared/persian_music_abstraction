Dastgah-e Shur Tuning Information
Source: https://tuning.ableton.com/persian-radif/dastgah-e-shur/

Scale Degrees and Functions
  First Degree (Aqaz): C
    Function: Beginning tone, starting point of improvisation
  Second Degree (Finalis/Foroud): D
    Function: Melodic cadence goal, conclusion tone
  Fifth Degree: G
  Sixth Degree (Moteghayyer): A
    Function: Variable/changeable note
    Variant: Can be lowered one quarter-tone to A koron

Musical Context
  Dastgah-e Shur features extensive repertoire in folklore and classical Iranian music
  and its vast sub-modes. It is considered the most essential Iranian musical mode.

  The sixth degree can be performed as either A natural or A koron (quarter-tone lowered),
  creating emphasis on the fifth degree (G).

Historical Notes
  Dastgah-e Shur has ancient roots and serves as a basis for regional Iranian folk music,
  though it was not historically among primary classical modes.
