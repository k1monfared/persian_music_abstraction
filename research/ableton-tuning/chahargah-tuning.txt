Dastgah-e Chahargah Tuning Information
Source: https://tuning.ableton.com/persian-radif/dastgah-e-chahargah/

General Description
  One of the most characteristic modes in Iranian classical music, described as having
  "vitality and strength" and considered suitable for epic music. It contrasts with Shur
  and Segah, which suit gentler, more sorrowful styles.

Scale Degrees and Functions
  First scale degree (Tonic): G
  Second scale degree: A koron (A with quarter-tone lowering)
    Function: Aqaz ("beginning"), the typical starting point for improvisation
  Fourth scale degree: C
    Function: Finalis/Foroud, the melodic cadence goal and conclusion tone

Technical Notes
  Uses the koron accidental, which lowers pitch by a quarter-tone
  (designed by Ali-Naqi Vaziri, 1886-1979)

Historical Context
  Part of the Persian Radif system of Iranian classical music, representing one of the
  twelve primary dastgahs in this modal tradition.
