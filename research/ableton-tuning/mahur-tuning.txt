Dastgah-e Mahur Tuning Information
Source: https://tuning.ableton.com/persian-radif/dastgah-e-mahur/

Basic Characteristics
  First scale degree: G
  Intervallic structure: Similar to Western Major mode
  Emotional content: Joyful
  Historical significance: Considered the most prominent Dastgah in Iranian classical music after Dastgah-e Shur

Scale Degrees and Functions
  Fourth degree (C): Aqaz, the beginning pitch where the Dastgah is typically initiated
  Fifth degree (D): Shahed, a prominent pitch during music creation

Special Performance Notes
  In certain cases, the higher B (but not the lower B) is played as Bb
  A dotted line indicates the area of melodic motion

Related Resources
  Musical example: Mohammad Reza Shajarian's "Sarve Chaman"

Note: This page emphasizes structural and functional aspects rather than providing
quantitative tuning data (no specific cent values or frequency ratios listed).
